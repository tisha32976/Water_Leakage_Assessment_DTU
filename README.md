# Water Leakage Assessment – DTU Campus

## Overview
This project focuses on assessing water leakage issues within the Delhi Technological University (DTU) campus. The study identifies major leakage zones, analyzes infrastructural causes, estimates water loss, and proposes sustainable solutions for efficient water management.

The project was carried out as part of an Environmental Engineering academic assessment.

---

## Objectives
- Identify major water leakage zones in DTU campus
- Analyze causes of water wastage
- Estimate environmental and financial impact
- Propose sustainable water management solutions
- Promote awareness regarding water conservation

---

## Methodology
- Field observations across campus
- Surveys and interviews with maintenance staff
- Data collection from leakage-prone areas
- Secondary research on smart water management systems

---

## Key Findings
### Major Leakage Zones
- Old Boys Hostel (OBH)
- Girls Hostel (GH-2)
- Mechanical Block
- Canteen Area
- Garden near Admin Block

### Estimated Water Loss
- Approx. **2400 litres/day**
- Estimated monthly loss: **₹3,600**

---

## Proposed Solutions
- IoT-based smart water meters
- GIS-integrated pipeline monitoring
- Automatic water shutoff systems
- Pipeline infrastructure upgrades
- Awareness campaigns for students and staff

---

## Impact
The study highlights how proper monitoring and sustainable infrastructure can significantly reduce water wastage and improve campus resource management.
